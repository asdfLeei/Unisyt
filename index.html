BSIT Course Diagnostic Matrix: Faculty SETE Rating vs. Class Failure Rate

Each point is one class section. High failure with low SETE suggests a teaching review; high failure with normal SETE may point to a rigorous subject that needs student tutoring. This shows association only, not causation. Click a point to open the section.

Responsible SETE use: check the response rate first; compare several semesters rather than one; SETE is never the sole measure of teaching effectiveness; only aggregated results are shown; class-level SETE is hidden when a class has fewer than 5 completed evaluations or a response rate below 30%.
